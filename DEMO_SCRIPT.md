# TerraLoop AI — 3–5 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem
“Most recycling apps answer one question: which bin? But the best environmental decision often happens before the bin. TerraLoop AI helps people decide whether an item should be repaired, reused, donated, recycled, or safely disposed.”

## 0:25–0:50 — Product
Show the landing page and scroll to the agent. Explain that the app runs fully in-browser with no account and no paid API.

## 0:50–1:45 — Live demo
Use this input:
- old laptop
- 3 plastic water bottles
- torn cotton t-shirt
- leftover food

Click “Build my circular plan.” Show the overall potential-diversion score, estimated avoided CO2e, and item count.

## 1:45–2:35 — Explainability
Open the recommendations visually and point out:
- the action chosen for each item;
- why the action ranks above disposal;
- the caution/local-policy note;
- the fact that the recommendation comes from a transparent rule match.

Use the battery example to show that TerraLoop does not put special waste into ordinary mixed recycling.

## 2:35–3:20 — Technology
Show `app.js` in the repository. Explain the inspectable knowledge base, natural-language pattern matching, quantity parsing, weighted diversion calculation, CO2e estimate aggregation, and HTML escaping. Emphasize that deterministic rules keep safety-sensitive disposal guidance explainable.

## 3:20–4:00 — Earth Forward impact
“TerraLoop shifts sustainability from sorting waste to preventing waste. The next version can connect to verified local repair shops, donation programs, and municipal recycling rules, creating an action layer for the circular economy.”

## 4:00–4:20 — Close
“Before it becomes waste, find its next best life. That’s TerraLoop AI.”

## Recording checklist
- Record at 1080p if available.
- Keep the browser zoom readable.
- Show one full successful analysis.
- Show the GitHub repository briefly.
- Do not exceed 5 minutes.
